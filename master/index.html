Questions

Things families ask us

Does Grandma need a smartphone?

No. Someone in the family adds her with a simple phone call.

Who can see our memories?

Only your family. Nothing is ever sold.

Does it work in Malayalam?

Yes. Everything works in English and Malayalam, and Muthassi can speak in whichever she likes.

What does it cost?

Joining the waitlist is free. If anything ever costs money, we’ll tell you clearly before you decide.

What if I don’t know much about my family history?

That’s fine. Start with one person and one story. The tree grows as the family adds to it.

Can family abroad join in?

Yes. Cousins in Germany or the Gulf see the same tree and can add memories from anywhere.
